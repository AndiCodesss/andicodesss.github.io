Privacy policy

Who uses it: only its owner, on the owner's own computer and the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses: the owner's Google Drive, to upload, list, download and delete the owner's own backup archives in one folder.

Where data goes: nowhere else. The access token stays on the owner's computer. No data is collected, stored on any server, sold or shared with third parties.

Revoking access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: 6 November 2026.
